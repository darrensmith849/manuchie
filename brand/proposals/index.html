Manuchie · identity proposals · 29 October 2026

A new mark,
three directions.

The gold italic “M” is retired. Below are three new identities for Manuchie (Emmanuel Manuchie Chiwanga), each with its own symbol, wordmark and horizontal lockup, all drawn as outlined vectors. Each is shown large, at favicon sizes (16, 32 and 48 px), on dark and light grounds, in the site’s header and as a round profile picture.

Each one rests on a different idea: light, an instrument, and a maker’s seal. All three stay on the page for reference; Hallmark is the one going ahead.

Chosen: 03 Hallmark

  • Chosen by the client on 29 October 2026. It is now being built into the site.
  • The full seal signs large work: record sleeves, merchandise, press and the profile picture.
  • The coursed-stone M carries the identity at small sizes: the header lockup, the favicon and the app icons.

01

Lumen

An M of two pillars, with a wedge of light falling between them.

The M is built from three solid pieces: two pillars, and between them a wedge of light cut free by two narrow rays. It comes from the worship-rays imagery of the “Been So Good” film, and from his name: Emmanuel, “God with us”, light that comes down into the middle of things.

It is also a downbeat, the single downward stroke a music director gives to bring a band in. Faith and music sit in the geometry, with no cross and no notes.

The wordmark is the site’s own serif, Newsreader, outlined and spaced as a logo. The dot of the i is replaced by a small falling wedge, so the symbol hides in the name. The wedge can also work on its own as a bullet, an end-card or a beam in motion graphics.

  • Colours Off-white pillars, terracotta light; black or white in one colour
  • Type Newsreader 520, optical size 60, outlined
  • 16 px Drawn pixel by pixel: 3 px pillars, 1 px rays, a stepped wedge
  • Status Proposal: symbol, wordmark and lockup

02

Mbira

An M played on five keys of Zimbabwe’s own keyboard.

Five keys pass over a bridge: the mbira, the instrument at the heart of Shona music for centuries and a keyboard in its own right, so the keyboardist’s instrument and his country meet in one shape. The outer keys are the stems of an M and the inner three trace its V, so the letter is played rather than written.

A gold bridge stands for the metal and the keys are off-white. The wordmark is Inter, widely spaced, for a clean, contemporary producer feel that sits apart from the serif of the site.

This is the most openly African and musical of the three. The risk is that, at a glance, it reads as an instrument icon (or a comb) before it reads as an M.

  • Colours Off-white keys, gold bridge (deep gold on light)
  • Type Inter 500, tracked +26%, outlined
  • 16 px Five 2 px keys under a 2 px bridge, all on whole pixels
  • Status Proposal: symbol, wordmark and lockup

03

Hallmark

Chosen

A maker’s seal, with the chevron of Great Zimbabwe running round it.

Like a craftsman’s hallmark, this seal signs the work. His full name, Emmanuel Manuchie Chiwanga, runs round the rim, and at its foot is the charge from Psalm 33:3 that the site already carries: “play skilfully”.

Inside it runs the chevron frieze from the wall of the Great Enclosure at Great Zimbabwe, the country’s masterwork of dry-stone building, which also reads as a continuous sound wave. At the centre, the M is laid like a course of stone between two bands.

The seal belongs on record sleeves, merchandise, certificates, press and the profile picture. Its lettering needs room, so below about 144 px the identity switches to the coursed-stone M on its own. That M is the mark in the header lockup, the favicon and the app icons.

  • Colours Muted gold on near-black; black and terracotta on light
  • Type Newsreader caps, outlined, set on the circle
  • 16 px Seal reduced to the stone M, drawn pixel by pixel

What’s in the folder

Hallmark is being wired into the site. brand/package/README.md has the usage rules and the exact tags to add.

Concepts

brand/concepts/<name>/: symbol.svg, wordmark.svg and lockup.svg for each concept, plus on-dark copies, small-size icons and a preview board.

Production package (Hallmark)

brand/package/: seal, M mark, wordmark and lockup in four colourways, favicon.svg / .ico, app and touch icons, a maskable icon, a social image and the web manifest.

Rebuild

brand/build.sh re-exports every PNG and ICO; --package lumen or --package mbira would rebuild the package from another concept.
